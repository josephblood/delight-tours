Our Premier Destinations

🦍

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Home to over half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas, Bwindi offers an unforgettable wildlife experience in an ancient rainforest.

  • Mountain gorilla trekking experiences
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • 120+ mammal species
  • 350+ bird species
  • Dense ancient rainforest
💧

Murchison Falls National Park

Uganda's largest national park features the spectacular Murchison Falls, where the mighty Nile explodes through a narrow gorge.

  • Dramatic Murchison Falls
  • Nile River boat safaris
  • Lions, elephants & giraffes
  • Hippos and crocodiles
  • Diverse wildlife viewing
🦁

Queen Elizabeth National Park

Uganda's most popular safari destination, famous for its tree-climbing lions and the stunning Kazinga Channel.

  • Tree-climbing lions in Ishasha
  • Kazinga Channel boat cruises
  • 95+ mammal species
  • 600+ bird species
  • Diverse ecosystems
🏔️

Rwenzori Mountains

The legendary "Mountains of the Moon" offer spectacular hiking and mountaineering adventures with snow-capped peaks near the equator.

  • Africa's third highest peak
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Unique alpine vegetation
  • Glaciers near the equator
  • Multi-day trekking routes
🏞️

Lake Bunyonyi

One of Africa's deepest lakes, surrounded by terraced hills and dotted with 29 islands, perfect for relaxation and water activities.

  • 29 scenic islands
  • Bilharzia-free swimming
  • Canoeing and boat tours
  • Traditional Batwa culture
  • Birdwatching paradise
🌊

Jinja & The Nile

The adventure capital of East Africa, home to the source of the Nile River and world-class white water rafting.

  • Source of the Nile
  • World-class rafting
  • Bungee jumping
  • Kayaking adventures
  • Sunset boat cruises
