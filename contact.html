Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 3-6 months in advance, especially for gorilla trekking as permits are limited. However, we can sometimes accommodate last-minute bookings depending on availability.

What's included in the tour price?

Our tours typically include accommodation, meals as specified, park entrance fees, activities, ground transportation, and an English-speaking guide. Gorilla/chimp permits, international flights, visas, and travel insurance are usually additional.

Do I need a visa for Uganda?

Most nationalities can obtain a visa on arrival at Entebbe Airport for $50 USD, or apply online in advance. We'll provide specific visa requirements based on your nationality.

What vaccinations do I need?

Yellow fever vaccination is mandatory and must be documented. We also recommend hepatitis A & B, typhoid, and malaria prophylaxis. Consult your doctor 4-6 weeks before travel.

Is Uganda safe for travelers?

Yes! Uganda is one of the safest countries in East Africa for tourists. Our experienced guides ensure your safety throughout the journey, and we monitor all situations carefully.

What's the best time to visit?

Uganda can be visited year-round. The dry seasons (June-September and December-February) offer easier trekking conditions, but the wet seasons have fewer tourists and lush landscapes.

Can you arrange custom tours?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating custom itineraries tailored to your interests, budget, and schedule. Contact us with your requirements and we'll design your perfect tour.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation policies vary by tour and season. Generally, gorilla permits are non-refundable, but we offer flexible policies for other components. Full details are provided with your booking confirmation.
